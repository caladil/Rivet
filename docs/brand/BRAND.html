Brand specification

Rivet's identity, as built. The mark is generated: make_mark.py is the source of truth and every SVG beside it is output. Typography and the neutrals are family constants shared with Athena, Olympus, Ignition, Tachyon, Trends, Lodestone and Reliquary; only the mark and the accent are Rivet's own.

Construction

Designed on a 100-unit grid, emitted at 6×. Three subpaths read under evenodd: the outer hexagon fills, the inner hexagon punches the field, and the disc sits in the punched field as the rivet.

orientation
Pointy-top (rotation 30°). A flat-top hexagon with a circle in it is the exact silhouette of a hex nut — a different fastener from a rivet. Pointy-top also stands taller than wide (77.9 × 88.4 design units), which centres better in a square tile and above the wordmark.
border
12 design units, uniform. Derived from the outer apothem, not the circumradius — offsetting the circumradius would leave the border thinner at the flats than at the corners.
field
10 units on the standard mark. The gap equals the border's own thickness, which is what stops the drawing reading as a ring with a speck in it.
rivet
34 units across (r = 17).
corner radius
5 units on the outer hexagon, scaled proportionally on the inner one.
emitted ink
467.7 units wide — inside the family's 400–800 band, so small_strk = 20 lands at 4.3% of the mark, against Athena's 479-unit owl at 4.17%.
fill rule
evenodd, and it is load-bearing. Under nonzero the field fills in and the mark becomes a solid slab with an invisible dot on it. Never normalise it against the wordmark's nonzero.
Verified numerically, not by eye. verify_mark.py samples 3 subpaths × 3000 points against the intended construction. Maximum boundary deviation 0.000042 design units — 0.0001% of the mark's width, which is 0.0000 px at a 512 px tile. Enclosed area 312 373.1 against an analytic 312 373.3 (0.0001%). The small variant scores identically. Hand-rolled arc maths produces plausible-looking but wrong shapes when a sweep flag is wrong, and the error is invisible at any size a human reviews at.
The splash artwork is a different asset and stays that way. assets/shield-512.rgba is two-tone — steel body, oxide rivet — and is drawn by cmd/rivet-splash and cmd/rivet-launcher. It cannot be the brand mark: build-brand.py recolours one fill and would flatten the second colour. This is the same constraint that stopped Ignition's charcoal-hexagon-plus-gradient-flame concept being taken as-is. The two are relatives, not versions of each other; changing one does not oblige a change to the other.

Small sizes — a different asset, not a downscale

appicon-small.svg is a white mark on a flat, full-bleed #20120D tile at 88% fill, emboldened by small_strk. It is the single source for .ico, .icns and every OS surface — those cannot switch on theme, and the opaque tile is exactly what makes one file work on both a light and a dark taskbar.

The small variant's first parameters were wrong, and nothing errored. It was first drawn thicker than the standard mark — border 13, rivet r 17 — on the instinct that small sizes want more weight. Rasterised at true 16 px, the centre row read: 
before  .+##+######+##+.   field gone — border welded to rivet
after   .+##.+####+.##+.   field survives as a full dark pixel
small_strk fattens every edge at once, so the field closes from both sides. The fix was the opposite of the instinct: give weight back — border 12, rivet r 15 — so the field starts wider. It now measures 11.97 units before embolden and 8.64 after, or 1.35 px at 16 px. Re-check by rasterising, never by eye in a preview pane, if these are ever touched.

Typography 04

Geist, variable, SIL OFL 1.1 — the family typeface, shared with every sibling project. The identity face does not change per project; that is what makes the projects read as one system.

wordmark
Geist weight 800, set as RIVET, baked to outlines.
descriptor
Geist weight 600, set as APPLIANCE OS, tracked out to lock its width to the wordmark's.
descriptor ratio
0.20 of the wordmark's size.
licence
OFL.txt sits beside Geist-var.ttf in this folder and must travel with it. The SIL OFL requires it, and it is an explicit acceptance criterion of Athena's adoption ticket 39ac7a — every adoption up to and including Trends missed it.
No shipped asset contains live text. Every glyph is an outline. The pre-existing assets this replaces specified Montserrat and had been silently rendering as Segoe UI on the maintainer's machine — a live <text> element renders differently on every machine and is a defect, not a convenience.

Why the accent has two values

#B0512F is light-biased: it holds comfortably on white and paper and dims on black. Left as the single accent it would put the family's weakest dark reading on the dark lockup's descriptor — which is small text, not a graphic. So accent carries a small lift and accent_on_light holds the ruled colour. This is the Olympus mechanism inverted: there the accent vanished on white (the retired #7BEEFF read ~1.35:1), here it dims on black.

Sibling values read from each project's own brand.json; a sibling with a monochrome identity holds no hue and cannot be collided with.

11.8° is closer than any accent this family has rejected, and it was ruled anyway. The three proposals killed in one pass for Trends sat at 7°, 8° and 10°; the jade that was accepted sat 32° from its nearest neighbour. Rivet at 11.8° clears check_color.py's 12° flag by 0.2 of a degree. The numbers were put to the maintainer before the colour was chosen and he ruled it: he did not care about crowding Ignition's color, because the two projects will live side by side (.rulings/ R-294).

This is a decision, not a missed check. It is recorded here, in brand.json's _comment, and in ADR-037, so that nobody re-opens it as an oversight. The supporting argument is the one the family already accepts for Ignition and Athena, which sit 9.1° apart and coexist: hue separation alone is not the test.

A muted, dark, corroded red-brown against a vivid flame orange: 8.3 points of lightness and 29.1 points of saturation apart. Verify against a real UI before treating it as settled — that is what the family record asks for, and it has not yet been done.

Brand colour is not UI semantics

Rivet already spends red, and the brand now competes with it. cmd/rivet-fleet/src/tui/theme.rs makes down() and error() Color::Red, and cmd/rivet-provision/src/field.rs carries a ruling built on "red at write time is a rejection, red already on the screen is a state you can see and fix." Those are semantic colours and must not be reassigned to decoration.

This is the same collision that pushed Trends off magenta — except there the resolution was to move the brand, and here the brand is staying. Rivet's oxide is separated from a terminal's Color::Red mostly by saturation, and a terminal palette is not under this project's control. Unverified: whether the fleet TUI's fault state remains unmistakable beside brand chrome, on a real terminal, at a real size. If it fails, move the semantic colour, not the accent.

Importing into Rivet 06

A · Copy the assets

Already done — this folder is docs/brand/. It regenerates standalone: make_mark.py, verify_mark.py, build-brand.py, Geist-var.ttf and OFL.txt are all vendored here. build-brand.py looks for the font beside itself first.

# regenerate everything, in order, from docs/brand/
uv run python make_mark.py
uv run --with svgelements python verify_mark.py
uv run --with uharfbuzz --with fonttools --with svgelements \
    python build-brand.py ./brand.json

Deterministic — same inputs give byte-identical output, so it is safe to run in CI as a drift check.

C · Still open

  • Nothing in the repository uses these assets yet. The README still has no logo header and no favicon has been generated. Adoption is a separate change.
  • The splash and launcher artwork is untouched. assets/shield-512.rgba remains the two-tone shield. Whether Rivet's boot screen should show the hexagon instead is an open question, not a decision this document makes — and it would be a change to a 1 MiB raw asset shared by two programs.
  • The Ignition proximity is unverified in use. The lightness/saturation argument above is measured; whether the two marks stay distinct side by side at 16 px is not.
  • The fleet TUI's red has not been re-checked against the brand. See section 05.
  • #B0512F must be added to the brand-kit skill's family table and to check_color.py's FAMILY dict. A project missing from that dict cannot be collided with — the omission has already produced one wrong answer (Reliquary) and one six-day gap for another sibling.
